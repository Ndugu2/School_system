# 👨‍🏫 Teacher Portal Showcase

The **Teacher Portal** is engineered for educators to streamline daily classroom operations, student attendance, marks capture, and blended learning management.

---

## 🔑 Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. Classroom & Attendance Management
- **Digital Roll Call**: Take fast attendance on mobile or web with status options: *Present*, *Absent*, *Late*, *Excused*.
- **Class Lists & Student Profiles**: Instant access to assigned class registers, subject enrollments, and emergency parent contacts.

### 2. Ugandan Curriculum Marks Capture
- **O-Level Formative & Summative Marks**: Enter Activity of Integration (AOI / 20%) and End of Term (EOT / 80%) scores.
- **A-Level Assessment**: Enter Paper marks with automated grade conversion (A through F) and subsidiary points.
- **Teacher Comments**: Provide individual progress remarks per student before submitting marks for HOD/Admin review.

### 3. Holiday LMS & Content Publishing
- **Course Modules**: Create digital learning modules with PDF attachments, notes, and video links.
- **Assignments & Quizzes**: Publish term-break assignments, set submission deadlines, and grade student submissions.
- **Discussion Forums**: Moderate class questions and academic threads.

### 4. Timetable & Schedule
- **Weekly Schedule**: Real-time view of teaching periods, room assignments, and subject allocation.
