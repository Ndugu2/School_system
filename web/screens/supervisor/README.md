# 🔍 Supervisor Portal Showcase

The **Supervisor Portal** is dedicated to Academic Directors, Heads of Department (HODs), Quality Inspectors, and Deputy Head Teachers to monitor school standards, academic integrity, student discipline, and campus welfare.

---

## 🔑 Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. Quality Assurance & Academic Supervision
- **Marks Verification & Approval**: Review subject mark entries submitted by teachers before final publishing.
- **Syllabus & Lesson Tracking**: Monitor course coverage, assessment pacing, and teacher attendance across streams.
- **Class Performance Analytics**: Comparative analysis of class averages, grade distributions, and outlier metrics.

### 2. Student Welfare & Behaviour Tracking
- **Disciplinary Incident Logging**: File incident reports with severity levels, investigative notes, and disciplinary actions taken.
- **Parent Notifications**: Automatic SMS/email alerts dispatched to parents upon logging critical behaviour incidents.
- **Clinic & Medical Logs**: Track health clinic visits, treatment logs, and medical clearances.

### 3. Facilities & Boarding Oversight
- **Hostel & Dormitory Inspections**: Log cleanliness, room conditions, and boarding facility evaluations.
- **Exeat Gate Pass Audit**: View and verify all active and returned student gate passes.
- **Campus Event Oversight**: Approve and schedule school events, sports galas, and parent-teacher consultative meetings.
