# 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent & Student Portal Showcase

The **Parent & Student Portal** empowers families with continuous, transparent access to attendance, academic growth, financial statements, and e-learning resources.

---

## 🔑 Key Features & Capabilities

### For Parents & Guardians
- **Live Attendance Monitoring**: Instant notifications and tracking for daily arrival, absences, lates, and excused leaves.
- **Academic Performance & Report Cards**: Access validated terminal report cards, subject score breakdowns, and teacher remarks.
- **Financial Account Statements**:
  - View invoices, total tuition/boarding fees, and outstanding balances in UGX.
  - One-click Mobile Money (MTN MoMo / Airtel Money) fee payments.
  - Download verified payment receipts.
- **Boarding Exeat Gate Passes**: Approve and receive secure OTP verification for student leave passes.
- **School Communication**: Receive school circulars, announcements, and consultative PTA meeting alerts.

### For Students
- **Holiday LMS E-Learning**: Access uploaded course modules, video lectures, and revision past papers.
- **AI Tutor Assistance**: 24/7 interactive curriculum support for Senior 1 – Senior 6 subjects.
- **Digital Library Catalog**: Search available physical textbooks, reference materials, and e-books.
- **Academic Results Hub**: Track individual subject progress and examination performance over terms.

---

## 📱 User Experience
- **Adaptive Layout**: Mobile-friendly PWA and responsive web layout optimized for smartphones and tablets.
- **Multi-Child Switcher**: Parents with multiple enrolled children can seamlessly toggle profiles from a single dashboard.
