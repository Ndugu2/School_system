# 🛡️ Admin Web Console Showcase

The **Admin Web Console** provides the central command center for school leaders (Head Teachers, Principals, Registrars, Bursars, and Super-Administrators) to oversee institutional operations, academics, finance, and human resources.

---

## 🔑 Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. Centralized Administration & Academic Setup
- **School Configuration**: Academic Year & Term lifecycle management (Term 1, Term 2, Term 3).
- **Class & Stream Structure**: Senior 1 through Senior 6 across all streams (East, West, North, Arts, Science).
- **User Governance**: Create, update, assign roles, and deactivate accounts for all staff, teachers, bursars, supervisors, parents, and students.

### 2. Admissions & Term Registration
- **Admissions Registry**: Capture student biodata, UNEB index numbers, PLE/UCE aggregates, and National LIN.
- **Requirement Verification**: Real-time checklist tracking for uniforms, books, medical sheets, and mathematical sets.
- **Class & Stream Allocation**: Automated student placement with capacity limits and class teacher assignments.

### 3. Ugandan Curriculum Grading & Examination Engine
- **NCDC O-Level (S1–S4)**: 20% Formative (AOI) + 80% Summative (EOT) Division (D1–F9) assessment.
- **UACE A-Level (S5–S6)**: 20-Point combination calculator with subsidiary points and principal passes.
- **Exam Results Approval**: Multi-tier review and approval workflow before publishing report cards.

### 4. Financial ERP & UGX Fee Management
- **Tuition & Boarding Invoices**: Automated billing per class and boarding status.
- **Payment Verification**: Reconcile Cash, Bank receipts, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, and SchoolPay transactions.
- **Defaulter & Collection Analytics**: Real-time monitoring of collected revenue vs outstanding balances.

### 5. Institutional Operations & Welfare
- **Asset & Stores Inventory**: Track school consumables and durable assets.
- **Hostel & Exeat Passes**: Parent OTP-verified exeat gate passes for boarding students.
- **Staff HR & Payroll**: Manage leave requests, attendance, and salary disbursements.
